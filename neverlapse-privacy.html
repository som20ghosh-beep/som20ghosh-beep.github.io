NeverLapse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

NeverLapse ("the app") helps you keep track of when your documents expire. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents stay on your phone, and the developer does not receive them.

Who is responsible

Som, contact: sd20venture@gmail.com.

Your documents and personal details stay on your device

  • The photos, scans, dates, notes and family member names you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, encrypted. They are never uploaded to the developer or to any server.
  • There is no account. You do not give the developer your name, email address or phone number.
  • Scanning and reading dates from a document (text recognition) is done on your phone. The text recognition models are part of the app, and the document scanner is provided by Google Play services and also works on the device.
  • The app lock PIN is never stored; only a salted hash of it is kept on the phone, and the optional fingerprint unlock is handled by Android.
  • The app asks for no storage, camera, contacts, SMS or location permission. It asks to show notifications so it can remind you.

Backups and PDF exports are your choice

If you make a backup, the app creates one file, encrypted with a password you choose, and hands it to the place you pick (for example Google Drive or your Downloads folder). If you export a document as a PDF, the PDF is handed to the app you pick in the share sheet. After that, the file is under the control of that service, not the developer. The developer cannot read your backup, and cannot recover it if you forget the password.

Advertising

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about your device and how the ads perform. This happens between your phone and Google. The developer does not receive your documents and does not receive this data.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app first shows a consent form from Google. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • More about how Google uses data from ad SDKs: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases and does not ask for payment details.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no account. The developer does not run a server for this app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and is meant for adults managing family papers.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Delete a document inside the app, use Forgot PIN? to erase everything, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. Backup and PDF files you saved elsewhere must be deleted where you saved them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date, and the app's listing on Google Play will point to it.
